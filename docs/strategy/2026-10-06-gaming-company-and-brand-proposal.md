# RealityDiffusion: gaming and computing direction proposal

Recorded: 6 October 2026. Status: proposal for review, not an adopted repositioning or release.
Source: Frank's visible Codex discussion on gaming, creative tools, hardware, research and naming.

## Intent

Explore whether RealityDiffusion can grow into a trustworthy interactive-computing venture connecting digital media, creation and engineering. The founder wants useful, considered products and a transformational identity, with a long-term research horizon.

Preserve RealityDiffusion's existing understanding, creation and verification mission and the Reality Check product. A documentation save does not replace the accepted product contract.

## Naming and narrative

RealityDiffusion suits the company/research conversation. Its length and association with diffusion models make a short consumer product name useful. Proposed relationship: Arcanea carries creative and gaming experiences; RealityDiffusion develops a carefully bounded engineering capability; Starlight retains its public developer/protocol and internal infrastructure roles.

Arcanea already has broad creative-platform positioning. The earlier idea of a separate RealityDiffusion Studio overlaps with it and should be evaluated as a capability serving an existing product, not an automatic new platform.

Candidate public descriptor for testing: "Tools for creating worlds and engineering the systems that run them."

Arcanea Build is a working setup-planning name. RealityDiffusion Labs is a candidate research descriptor. Neither is an adopted identity.

## Product opportunities to compare

1. A grounded gaming/creator setup plan from budget, region, existing machine and desired workloads.
2. An editable playable-world workflow with permitted assets, engine export, performance evidence and recovery.
3. Dimensioned original accessories with CAD export and real prototype validation.

Compare serious alternatives on the same task: existing configurators for setup planning; Sloyd, Promethean AI and engine workflows for creation. Measure useful output, repair effort, time, cost and recovery. Do not infer demand from an internal demonstration.

## Engineering direction

Use typed constraints and grounded catalogues for compatibility and prices. Models interpret intent and explain tradeoffs; product facts and budgets need independent validation.

Godot/Blender, Unreal, glTF/OpenUSD, parametric CAD, local runtime and bounded cloud jobs are candidates to verify, not commitments to adopt every dependency. Existing accepted implementations should be reused.

Useful research includes reversible scene operations, performance under multiple workloads, permitted agent-based game testing, reproducible benchmarks and provenance/recovery.

## Prospective relationships

NVIDIA/AMD are compute candidates; ASUS ROG and appropriate distributors are hardware candidates; Genba/Ztorm serve authorised game distribution. Steam, Xbox and PlayStation developer routes concern original-game publishing. No agreements are established by this document.
