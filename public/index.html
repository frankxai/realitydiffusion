Reality Diffusion

Reality Check

An evidence protocol for deciding what you can responsibly say about a photograph, a clip, or a voice note. It runs in this browser tab. The media never leaves your device, because it never enters this page.

  • No upload. You describe what you examined; you never hand over the file.
  • No detector. This product runs no model on your media and makes no detection claim.
  • No verdict. Every claim comes out as a banded lean with a ceiling, an alternative explanation, and the thing that would overturn it.

1 The case

Write down what you are looking at and how it reached you, before you look for anything. Chain of custody outranks every judgement you can make about pixels.

2 The claim under test

Not "is this AI". A claim you could be wrong about in a way someone could show: that the scene was not captured where and when it is said to have been, that the material was generated rather than recorded, that a real recording has been materially altered.

3 The checklist

Answer only what you actually checked. Unknown is a real answer and it is scored as one: it dilutes the result rather than being quietly dropped. Each question carries the innocent explanations that produce the same observation.

4 Alternative explanations

Every innocent account of what you observed, carried forward. Closing one requires a reason. While any remain open, the reading is capped below its top band — deliberately.

Answer a question above and the alternative explanations that come with it will appear here.

The decision is about what you will do, not about what is true.

What this is not

  
Reality Diffusion does not claim reliable detection of AI-generated media, and this page performs none. Detection claims require measured error rates on media resembling the open web; the evidence that would have to exist before we made one is written down in BENCHMARK-PLAN.md in the source repository.

  
Reality Check is separate from Reality Architect, which is about personal operating methods, and from Arcanea, which is about creative work. This surface has one job.
